2. deal: hero `split` (first deal `film` dealt again: the firm has no film) · palette `cobalt-on-sand`:
   a sand ground like ledger paper and one cobalt ink, the two colours of finance paperwork, the
   firm's first sector · type `hanken` (Hanken Grotesk; the second pairing printed, taken because the
   first, Newsreader, is the serif of the last site built on this machine, for a law firm) · radius

   sets a short name in the middle) · footer `footer-columns` · close `close-type` · rhythm dealt

   page ground it read as an empty screen; the cobalt band is its picture · buttons `block` · mark
   `stack` · moment `word-highlight` in #firm.
   Stack: Vite + React 19 + TypeScript, prerendered to static HTML at build; the kit in public/assets.

Voice: facts only (copy-registers.md 1), second person in the contact zone
Pairs: plain, not curt; exact, not cold
Person: third person for the firm; "you" only in the contact section
Sentences: 8 to 20 words
Humour: none
House words: the listing's own nouns: strategic planning, operational support, project management,
  startups, SMEs, NGOs, capacity, internal processes, sustainable growth
Never: virtue words, invented clients, figures, people, prices or a response-time promise

Main action: Request a meeting (#meeting). Secondary in the hero: See the services (#services).
